# The Standard Wins — and How to Change It

A standard is a promise the team makes to its future self, and like all
promises it only holds if it can be renegotiated in good faith.

If the toolchain or the team shows a better way, the doctrine changes *on
purpose*: a proposal, a review, a decision recorded in chapter 11. What
the standard forbids is drift — the quiet divergence that happens when
nobody decides anything and the codebase decides for you.

This book is a snapshot of the standard at a moment in time. The repo is
the living document: <https://github.com/staylorx/dart-flutter-bible>.
When the two disagree, the standard wins.
